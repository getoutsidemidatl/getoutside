Cape Henlopen Sunset Kayak Tour

Date & Time

Weekend evenings

Check tour schedule

Location

Cape Henlopen State Park, Lewes, DE

Cost

Paid guided tour; rentals available

Best For

Beginners, couples, nature lovers

A no-experience-needed sunset paddle on the Delaware Bay side of the park, launching from the fishing pier. Dolphins, osprey, rays, and skates are common summer sightings, with lighthouse views the whole way.

📍

Parking

Park entrance lot; follow signs to the fishing pier

📏

Distance

~2 hours from Philadelphia

📞

Contact

Check destateparks.com for tour times
