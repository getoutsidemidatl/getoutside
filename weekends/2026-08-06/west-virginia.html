New River Gorge Whitewater Rafting

Date & Time

Weekend

Daylight hours

Location

New River Gorge National Park & Preserve, Fayetteville, WV

Cost

$50–$120 depending on outfitter

Best For

Adventure seekers, thrill-seekers

World-class whitewater in one of the East Coast's most scenic gorges. August water levels suit a range of trip intensities, from family floats to bigger rapids on the Lower New.

📍

Parking

Available at outfitter base camps

📏

Distance

~1 hour from Charleston, WV

📞

Summersville Lake — Kayaking & Swimming

Date & Time

Weekend

9 AM – sunset

Location

Summersville Lake, WV

Cost

Free parking; rentals $30–$50

Best For

Families, paddlers, swimmers

Clear mountain-lake water, sandstone cliffs, and calm coves make Summersville one of WV's best swimming and paddling spots — a good lower-key pairing with a rafting day nearby.

📍

Parking

Free at multiple public access points

📏

Distance

~45 minutes from Charleston

📞

Harpers Ferry — History & Hiking

Date & Time

Weekend

9 AM – 5 PM

Location

Harpers Ferry National Historical Park, WV

Cost

$8 park entrance (7-day pass)

Best For

History buffs, families, hikers

Combine a walk through Civil War-era history with a stretch of the Appalachian Trail and river views at the confluence of the Potomac and Shenandoah. Easy to pair with a half-day hike.

📍

Parking

Visitor center lot, with shuttle into town

📏

Distance

~1.5 hrs from Charleston, ~1 hr from DC

📞

Contact

Harpers Ferry NPS (304) 535-6029
