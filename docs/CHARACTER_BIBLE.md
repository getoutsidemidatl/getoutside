# Get Outside Mid Atlantic — Character Bible & Prompt Library

Locked visual system for featured website cards and social carousels.
**Style:** Grok Imagine · construction-paper / South Park deadpan aesthetic.
**Rule:** Original characters only. Never use real South Park names, designs, or IP.

---

## Core Style Prompt (always prepend)

```
South Park style construction paper cutout animation, flat solid colors, bold thick black outlines, minimal to no shading, simple geometric shapes, round heads, tiny eyes and mouth, deadpan blank expression, clean vector look, no realism, no detailed textures, bright saturated palette
```

## The Crew

### Dan — The Steady One
- **Look:** Green beanie (always on), orange life jacket over simple gray t-shirt, khaki cargo shorts, brown hiking shoes, short brown hair peeking from beanie, slight freckles optional, deadpan face.
- **Personality:** Practical, calm, slightly exasperated by the others’ chaos. The one who checks the map and brings extra snacks.
- **Signature prop:** Folded map or compass in hand, or life jacket zipper half done.
- **Prompt snippet:**
  ```
  Dan, young adult male, green beanie, orange life jacket, khaki shorts, short brown hair, deadpan expression, standing confidently
  ```

### Kevin — The Disaster Magnet
- **Look:** Messy dark brown hair, soaked or damp light blue hoodie (often with water stains), cargo shorts, one sandal missing or broken paddle in hand, slightly hunched, deadpan but resigned face.
- **Personality:** Somehow always wet, injured, or missing a shoe. Never complains. Pure chaos magnet.
- **Signature prop:** Broken paddle, dripping water, bandage on forehead, or missing shoe.
- **Prompt snippet:**
  ```
  Kevin, young adult male, messy dark hair, soaked light blue hoodie, cargo shorts, holding a broken paddle, dripping wet, deadpan resigned expression
  ```

### Riley — The Outdoor Optimist
- **Look:** Curly light brown / dirty-blond hair, olive green fleece pullover, dark hiking pants, sturdy boots, often holding a marshmallow stick or trail map, bright but still deadpan eyes.
- **Personality:** Sees the best in every trail, every weather, every detour. The one who starts the campfire songs (badly).
- **Signature prop:** Marshmallow roasting stick, binoculars, or a small backpack.
- **Prompt snippet:**
  ```
  Riley, young adult with curly light brown hair, olive green fleece, dark hiking pants, holding a marshmallow stick, deadpan but hopeful expression
  ```

### Jax — The Focused Paddler
- **Look:** Blond hair under red baseball cap (backwards or forwards), blue life vest, board shorts, water shoes, paddle in hand, athletic build, focused deadpan stare.
- **Personality:** All about the water. Talks stroke technique. The one who actually knows the put-in.
- **Signature prop:** Kayak paddle, red cap, or a small dry bag.
- **Prompt snippet:**
  ```
  Jax, young adult male, blond hair under red baseball cap, blue life vest, board shorts, holding a kayak paddle, focused deadpan expression
  ```

## Reusable Scene Templates

**Group dock shot (hero / about):**
```
[style prompt], four characters standing side by side on a wooden dock: Dan in green beanie and orange life jacket, Kevin in soaked blue hoodie holding broken paddle, Riley in olive fleece with marshmallow stick, Jax in red cap and blue life vest with paddle, calm water and distant trees in background, bright daylight, deadpan faces looking at camera
```

**Festival / Fair card:**
```
[style prompt], [Character] at a colorful outdoor fair, carnival lights and tents in background, [activity], deadpan expression, bright sunny day
```

**Water / Kayak card:**
```
[style prompt], [Character] in kayak or on dock with paddle, calm river or bay water, green trees on shore, bright sky, deadpan expression
```

**Campfire / Trail card:**
```
[style prompt], [Character] by a small campfire or on a trail, evening light or forest, marshmallows or map, deadpan expression
```

**Portrait reference (square for profile / stamp):**
```
[style prompt], close-up portrait of [Character description], plain solid color background, centered, deadpan expression, high contrast outlines
```

## Generation Rules (cost control)

1. Pre-generate only high-value assets (featured weekend cards, hero images, social carousels that will be reused).
2. Store final PNGs/JPGs in `/assets/characters/` or `/assets/featured/` once approved.
3. Reuse the same character portraits across multiple cards by compositing or simple background swaps when possible.
4. Bulk weekly cards continue to use the free SVG `scene-generator.js`.
5. Always include the core style prompt + exact character snippet for consistency.
