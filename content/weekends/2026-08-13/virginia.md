## Hidden Gem

**Great Country Farms — Big Dig + Big Rigs**  
**Region:** Virginia  
**Date/Time:** August 15–16, 2026  
**Location:** Great Country Farms, Bluemont, VA  
**Category:** Outdoor / Family farm  
**Cost:** Check farm site  
**Best For:** Families, adventure  
**Source:** [The Fairfax Girl / summer events](https://thelocalgirl.com/fairfax/august-2026-events-northern-virginia/)

**Description:**  
Potato dig in the fields plus touch-a-truck (tractors, construction vehicles, fire engines). Loudoun countryside, not another downtown festival.

**Why it's a gem:** The listing sites push Arlington Fair and Wolf Trap; this is the actual “kids will talk about it Monday” option an hour west.

**Logistics:**
- **Parking:** On-site
- **Distance:** ~50 miles from DC
- **Contact:** greatcountryfarms.com

**Social Post Fit:** Instagram, Facebook
