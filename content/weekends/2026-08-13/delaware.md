**Region:** Delaware  
**Date/Time:** Saturday, August 15, 2026 — 10 AM–3 PM  
**Location:** 1301 Heritage Rd, Dover AFB, DE  
**Category:** Outdoor / Museum  
**Cost:** Free (check base access rules)  
**Best For:** Families, adventure  
**Source:** [amcmuseum.org](https://www.amcmuseum.org/events/open-cockpit-day/)

**Logistics:**
- **Parking:** Museum lot; confirm gate/ID requirements

- **Contact:** amcmuseum.org
