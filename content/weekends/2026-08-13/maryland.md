## Pick #1: Montgomery County Agricultural Fair

**Region:** Maryland  
**Date/Time:** August 14–22, 2026 (weekend Aug 15–16 is peak)  
**Location:** 501 Perry Parkway, Gaithersburg, MD  
**Category:** Festival / Fair  
**Cost:** Gate fee (check fair site); rides extra  
**Best For:** Families, adventure seekers  
**Source:** [Montgomery County Agricultural Fair](https://www.mcagfair.com/) / [Baltimore Banner festival guide](https://www.thebanner.com/culture/things-to-do/2026-maryland-festival-list-music-art-food-R4F3MRTNHJCDLLFOSYJLPWUPYM/)

**Description:**  
Livestock, carnival rides, live music, and classic fair food. Founded 1949 — still one of the biggest agricultural weekends in the DMV.

**Logistics:**
- **Parking:** On-site (paid); shuttles often available
- **Distance:** ~25 miles from DC / ~35 from Baltimore
- **Contact:** mcagfair.com

**Social Post Fit:** Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, X

**Region:** Maryland  
**Date/Time:** Friday, August 14, 2026 — 5:30–6:30 PM  
**Location:** Irvine Nature Center, Owings Mills area, MD  
**Category:** Outdoor / Nature  
**Cost:** Check Irvine for registration  
**Best For:** Families, couples
