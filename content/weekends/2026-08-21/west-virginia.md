## Hidden Gem

**Daytime free stages + DockDogs or quieter mountain trail**  
**Region:** West Virginia  
**Date/Time:** Friday–Saturday daytime, Aug 21–22  
**Location:** State Fair grounds (daylight circuit) or New River Gorge / nearby trail options  
**Category:** Outdoor / Family  
**Cost:** Gate admission only (or free trail access)  
**Best For:** Families, reset  
**Source:** State Fair schedule + research lock

**Description:**  
Skip the expensive grandstand seat for a few hours — DockDogs, free stages, and livestock barns are where the fair still feels local. Or leave the grounds for a short mountain trail hour.

**Why it's a gem:** Most out-of-state coverage only lists the headliners. The free daylight circuit (or a quiet trail) is the actual “we should do this every year” part.

**Logistics:**
- **Parking:** Fair lots or trailheads
- **Distance:** Lewisburg / New River area
- **Contact:** statefairofwv.com or NPS New River Gorge

**Social Post Fit:** Instagram, Facebook
