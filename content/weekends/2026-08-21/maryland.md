## Pick #1: Montgomery County Agricultural Fair (final days)

**Region:** Maryland  
**Date/Time:** August 14–22, 2026 — Fri–Sat Aug 21–22 are the closer  
**Location:** 501 Perry Parkway, Gaithersburg, MD  
**Category:** Festival / Fair  
**Cost:** Gate fee (check fair site); rides extra  
**Best For:** Families, adventure seekers  
**Source:** [mcagfair.com](https://www.mcagfair.com/) / Baltimore Banner festival coverage

**Description:**  
Livestock, carnival rides, live music, demos, and classic fair food. “Barn in the USA” theme. Founded 1949 — still one of the biggest agricultural weekends in the DMV. Last full days this weekend.

**Logistics:**
- **Parking:** On-site (paid); free shuttle options from Montgomery College campuses often available
- **Distance:** ~25 miles from DC / ~35 from Baltimore
- **Contact:** mcagfair.com
