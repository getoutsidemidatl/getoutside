## Pick #3: James River Paddling & Tubing

**Region:** Virginia
**Date/Time:** Weekend, daylight hours (outfitters typically run 9 AM–4 PM)
**Location:** James River, Richmond, VA
**Category:** Boating/Paddling
**Cost:** $30–$60 depending on outfitter and trip length
**Best For:** Families, beginners, a good lower-exertion alternative to hiking
**Source:** [James River Association — Outfitters Guide](https://thejamesriver.org/james-river-outfitters-kaykak-tubing-boat-guided-trips/)

**Description:**
Richmond-area outfitters (Riverside Outfitters, RVA Paddlesports, KingFish Boat Rentals) run guided and self-service kayak, tube, and flatwater trips right through the city. The James River Association posts a weekly water-conditions update every Friday through Labor Day.

**Logistics:**
- **Parking:** Available at outfitter locations and Forest Hill Park / Pony Pasture access points
- **Distance:** Central Richmond
- **Contact:** Check thejamesriver.org for current "James River Watch" conditions before you go

**Social Post Fit:** Instagram, TikTok, Facebook

## Pick #4: Blue Ridge Parkway & Shenandoah Hiking

**Region:** Virginia
**Date/Time:** Weekend, daylight hours
**Location:** Shenandoah National Park & Blue Ridge Parkway, VA
**Category:** Hiking
**Cost:** $30 per vehicle (Shenandoah 7-day pass); Parkway is free
**Best For:** All skill levels — check AQI first (see note above)
**Source:** [Blue Ridge Parkway — Hiking Trails](https://www.blueridgeparkway.org/things-to-do/hiking/)

**Description:**
Shenandoah's 500+ miles of trails include family-friendly options like Stony Man (1.6 miles round trip) up through the demanding Old Rag ascent. The Parkway adds waterfall walks and overlook hikes along its milepost markers.

**Logistics:**
- **Parking:** Multiple lots throughout the park; arrive before 9 AM on weekends
- **Distance:** ~90 minutes from Washington DC
- **Contact:** Shenandoah NPS (540) 999-3500

**Social Post Fit:** Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, X
