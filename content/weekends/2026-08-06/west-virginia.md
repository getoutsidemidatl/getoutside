## Pick #1: New River Gorge Whitewater Rafting

**Region:** West Virginia
**Date/Time:** Weekend, daylight hours
**Location:** New River Gorge National Park & Preserve, Fayetteville, WV
**Category:** Water Activity
**Cost:** $50–$120 depending on outfitter and trip
**Best For:** Adventure seekers, thrill-seekers
**Source:** [Adventures on the Gorge](https://www.adventuresonthegorge.com/)

**Description:**
World-class whitewater in one of the East Coast's most scenic gorges. August water levels suit a range of trip intensities, from family floats to bigger rapids on the Lower New.

**Logistics:**
- **Parking:** Available at outfitter base camps
- **Distance:** ~1 hour from Charleston, WV
- **Contact:** Adventures on the Gorge (855) 379-8738

**Social Post Fit:** Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, X

## Pick #2: Summersville Lake — Kayaking & Swimming

**Region:** West Virginia
**Date/Time:** Weekend, 9 AM–sunset
**Location:** Summersville Lake, WV
**Category:** Water Activity
**Cost:** Free parking; rentals $30–$50
**Best For:** Families, paddlers, swimmers
**Source:** [Summersville Lake](https://www.summersvillelake.com/)

**Description:**
Clear mountain-lake water, sandstone cliffs, and calm coves make Summersville one of WV's best swimming and paddling spots — a good lower-key pairing with a rafting day nearby.

**Logistics:**
- **Parking:** Free at multiple public access points
- **Distance:** ~45 minutes from Charleston
- **Contact:** Summersville Lake Association (304) 872-3282

**Social Post Fit:** Instagram, TikTok

## Pick #3: Harpers Ferry — History & Hiking

**Region:** West Virginia
**Date/Time:** Weekend, 9 AM–5 PM
**Location:** Harpers Ferry National Historical Park, WV
**Category:** Historic Site
**Cost:** $8 park entrance (7-day pass)
**Source:** [National Park Service — Harpers Ferry](https://www.nps.gov/hafe/index.htm)
**Best For:** History buffs, families, hikers

**Description:**
Combine a walk through Civil War-era history with a stretch of the Appalachian Trail and river views at the confluence of the Potomac and Shenandoah. Easy to pair with a half-day hike.

**Logistics:**
- **Parking:** Visitor center lot, with shuttle into town
- **Distance:** ~1.5 hours from Charleston, ~1 hour from DC
- **Contact:** Harpers Ferry NPS (304) 535-6029

**Social Post Fit:** Instagram, Facebook
